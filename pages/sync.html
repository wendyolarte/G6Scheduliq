Sync & Integrations

Export your schedule to Google Calendar and manage course links for Zoom and Google Classroom. Provider accounts are not connected to Scheduliq.

📆 Google Calendar

Add one event at a time with its Google Calendar button, or export the selected events as one calendar file. Exports are one-way: changes in Google Calendar will not sync back to Scheduliq.

Open Google Calendar import settings
  1. Choose the start date, number of weeks, and which events to include.
  2. Download the calendar file above.
  3. In Google Calendar, open Settings → Import & export → Import, select the downloaded .ics file, then choose the destination calendar.

You can also add a single event below. Google Calendar opens with its details filled in; sign in there if prompted, then save the event.
