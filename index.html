Transparent about data and limitations

These notes describe the current implementation; they are not a formal privacy policy.

Accounts and session handling

  • Passwords are stored as bcrypt hashes, not as readable passwords.
  • The sign-in token expires after eight hours and is kept in browser storage so the app can maintain the session.
  • Account profiles and scheduling records are stored in the MySQL database configured for the service.

Use a test account for demonstrations and sign out on shared devices. Do not enter sensitive information that the scheduling workflow does not need.

Review before relying on a schedule

  • Schedule generation uses heuristic search and does not guarantee a globally optimal timetable.
  • Conflict results and suggested alternatives should be reviewed by faculty and administrators.
  • Campus floor layouts and sample room details are not verified official floor plans.

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about accounts, schedules, and campus information.

Who can create an account?

Students and faculty/staff can register. Admin accounts are created by an existing administrator and cannot be self-registered from the public sign-up form.

Does the schedule generator guarantee that every conflict is eliminated?

No. It searches for high-scoring schedule options and checks configured constraints. Review the results and conflict warnings before a schedule is approved.

Does Scheduliq automatically sync my Google, Zoom, or Classroom account?

No. Calendar export and event links are user-initiated, and changes do not sync back automatically. Zoom and Classroom links and sharing are managed manually.

Are the campus floors and room capacities official?

The supplied map is shown as a campus reference. Sample floors, rooms, capacities, and equipment are illustrative placeholders; verify them with campus staff before relying on them.

How are passwords handled?

The backend stores bcrypt password hashes and checks sign-in passwords against those hashes. Do not share your password or display it in a public demo.

Where can I get account help?

Contact the Scheduliq site administrator who provided your account. The built-in assistant can help with general navigation questions.
